4. Define Key Query Questions
Examples: + how to construct
    •    What resources are used by a deployment?
    Join deployment with deployment-resource and resource to retrieve  
    resources associated with each deployment, including resourceId, assignedAt and releasedAt.
    •    Who is paying on behalf of who?
    Join beneficiary to paying_party to client to find the organization paying for their usage.
    Join paying_party to beneficiary to client to find all the beneficiaries (employees) that the paying party is paying for.
    •    What substitute deployments are available?
    Find resources of a specific type (join by x region, y performanceTier) are available by joining resource_type and resource.
    •    What usage occurred over a time period?
    Find usagerecord, where starttime and endtime fall within the time period. possible to also limit to a single entity by joining to client table.
    •    What bills were generated for a client?
    Given clientId, join to client to beneficiary to bill_item to bill_header table and print out all billids (and relevant bill data) where the paying party (paying_party) is that client.
    •    What payments have been made?
    Given bill id find all payments that correponed to the billid with a join to bill from payment.
    •    What infrastructure resources are available?
    Query resource where currentStatus = 'available', and exclude any resources with active resource_availability_event windows whose startTime <= targetTime <= endTime
    •    Which deployment is currently running?
    Find deployment_status where statusName is "ongoing". Select the statusId and join deployment table
    •    5. How does actual assignment differ from the requested reservation region?
    Join reservation to actual_assignment, then join both region IDs to cloud_region to obtain readable region names.
    •    What time did the client request for reservation?
    Given client, find requestStartTime and requestEndTime under the reservation table
